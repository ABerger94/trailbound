# Trailbound — Full Walkthrough & Solutions

*Companion to `game.html`. Spoilers ahead — every trail, step by step.*

**How to play (quick reference)**

- **Move:** WASD / arrows, left stick, or tap-to-move. **Switch character:** Tab (or tap the Switch button / press S).
- **Act:** E / Space / tap or B-button — talk, dig, pull levers, drop rope, throw stick, bark, collect.
- **Whistle (Theo):** T / Y-button — calls Pip to follow or stay.
- **Goal:** get **both** Theo and Pip onto the campsite (tent) tile. Levels 4–5 also need the relic/compass; in Pack 2, Trails 7, 8 and 10 need their relic/marker too.

**Characters:** Theo (ranger, orange coat) walks trails, climbs ladders, pulls levers, drops rope from anchors, throws sticks, reads signs. Pip (cream dog) squeezes through tunnels, swims ponds, digs mounds, barks to distract the badger, follows Theo or stays on whistle.

**The badger:** naps near its den. Wakes if Theo comes within ~2.5 tiles with clear sight (blocked by trees). It chases at just under Theo's speed — if it touches him, Theo is carried back to the trailhead (keeps key/relic). Lure it away with Pip's bark (5-tile range), then slip past; while it's curious, sniffing, or waddling home it ignores Theo.

## Trail 1 — "First Steps"

*A gentle meadow. Learn to walk, switch, and camp.*

1. Walk Theo (start: west trailhead) east along the path, around the pond, to the campsite tent at the far east.
2. Press **Tab** to switch to Pip, and walk her to the same tent.
3. When both stand on the campsite, the trail completes.

*Fireflies: 3 along the path. Nothing can hurt you here.*

---

## Trail 2 — "The Old Gate"

*A lever behind the trees opens the way east.*

1. Switch to **Pip**. Take the tunnel (dark hole) just north of the pond — only Pip fits.
2. On the far side, walk to the lever (brass handle) and press **Act** — the stone door in the east wall rumbles open.
3. Walk Pip south through the now-open door to the campsite.
4. Switch to **Theo**. Walk east through the open door to the campsite.
5. Both on the tent — trail complete.

*Fireflies: 3 (two near the pond, one by the gate). The vertical wall north of the pond means the lever really is Pip's job.*

---

## Trail 3 — "Over the Falls"

*Theo can't cross the waterfall — but he can rig a rope for Pip.*

1. As **Theo**, walk to the metal anchor ring bolted to the cliff at the waterfall's edge.
2. Press **Act** — Theo ties off and drops a rope down the cliff face.
3. Switch to **Pip**. Climb the rope ladder (walk into it) up onto the high ledge.
4. Walk Pip east past the pines to the campsite.
5. Switch to **Theo** and walk him east to the tent. Trail complete.

*Fireflies: 4 (one by the pines, one at the pool, two near the trailhead). Pip can't reach the ledge without the rope — swimming the pool doesn't help.*

---

## Trail 4 — "The Badger's Den"

*A grumpy badger naps by its den, guarding the passage north to the relic.*

1. Switch to **Pip** and walk her to the boulder just south of the napping badger.
2. Press **Act** to **bark** — the badger wakes, gets curious, and waddles over to sniff where Pip barked.
3. Switch to **Theo**. Slip north through the now-unguarded passage (between the pines).
4. Walk east along the ridge, past the signs, to the stone arch and take the **old relic** (walk into it).
5. Walk Theo east and south, around the pond, to the campsite tent at the far east.
6. Switch to **Pip**, walk her to the tent too. With both on the campsite **and** the relic in hand — trail complete.

*Fireflies: 4. If the badger catches Theo, he's carried back to the trailhead but keeps the relic — just lure the badger again. The badger ignores Theo entirely while it's curious, sniffing, or heading home.*

---

## Trail 5 — "The Compass Vault"

*Everything you've learned, in one trail: scent, digging, a key, a gate, a badger, a tunnel, a lever, a rope, and the compass.*

1. **Follow the scent.** Switch to **Pip** and sniff out the glowing scent trail in the southwest. Follow it to the cracked dirt **mound**.
2. **Dig.** Press **Act** on the mound — Pip digs up a **brass key**.
3. **Unlock the gate.** Switch to **Theo**, pick up the key, and walk east to the **iron gate**. Press **Act** while pushing into it to unlock it with the key.
4. **Distract the badger.** Switch to **Pip**, walk to the log east of the gate, and **bark** (Act). The badger leaves its post to investigate.
5. **Tunnel to the lever.** While the badger is busy, slip Pip into the **tunnel** south of the badger's den. It comes out in the north grove.
6. **Open the stone door.** Walk Pip to the **lever** and pull it (Act) — the stone door in the north wall opens.
7. **Through the door.** Walk Pip back south through the now-open door (or leave her — she can follow later). Switch to **Theo** and walk him east past the old gate, through the open stone door, to the north grove.
8. **Drop the rope.** As Theo, climb the **ladder** onto the high plateau and walk to the **anchor** at the cliff edge. Press **Act** — the rope drops down the cliff.
9. **Up the rope.** Switch to **Pip** and climb the rope to the plateau.
10. **Take the compass.** Walk Theo to the **old compass** on its stone dais and pick it up.
11. **Camp.** Walk both Theo and Pip to the **campsite** tent in the southeast corner. With the compass in hand and both friends on the tent — the adventure ends.

*Fireflies: 4. The badger can't climb ladders — the plateau is safe ground. If it catches Theo, he keeps the key and compass and restarts at the trailhead.*

---

## Trail 6 — "Stone and Weight"

*The Grey Peaks begin with a lesson in weight: copper doors, stone plates, and something heavy to hold them.*

1. **Feel the plates.** Walk Theo (start: west trailhead) to the stone plate at (9,4) and stand on it — the copper door at (10,6), set in the rock wall to the south, swings open. Step off and it slams shut: a plate only holds while something presses it.
2. **Push the crate.** Walk Theo around to (12,4), east of the heavy crate at (11,4). Push west twice — the crate slides (11,4) → (10,4) → (9,4), landing on the plate. The copper door swings open and *stays* open. (Pip can't push crates — she's moral support on this one.)
3. **Through the door.** Walk Theo south through the held-open door, past the fireflies, to the campsite tent at (10,9).
4. Press **Tab** to switch to Pip (she starts at (5,1)) and walk her south through the still-open door to the same tent. Both on the campsite — trail complete.

*Fireflies: 2 — at (7,8) and (13,8), south of the door.*

---

## Trail 7 — "The Frozen Path"

*A frozen lake bars the way. Something glints on an island mid-lake — one of Grandfather's old trail markers. Theo can't walk on ice. He can only slide.*

1. **Read the sign** at (9,1): Theo slides in a straight line until he reaches a shore or an island — plan each slide. New input steers him, so commit to a line.
2. **Cross the lake.** Walk Theo to the north shore and slide south down any column — he'll glide across the frozen rows (rows 4–7) and stop on the south shore. Walk him east along the shore toward the campsite side. (Pip is sure-footed — she just walks across the ice, no sliding.)
3. **The island marker.** The old trail marker sits on a tiny island at (12,5), ringed by ice on every side. Walk Theo to the south shore at (12,8), face north, and slide — he'll glide up column 12 and stop on the island, picking up the marker. (A lone pine at (12,4) blocks the north approach — the south slide is the only way in.)
4. **Camp.** Walk Theo around the dry east side of the lake and Pip straight across to the campsite tent at (15,8). With the marker in hand and both friends on the tent — trail complete.

*Fireflies: 2 — one on the ice at (6,6) (slide down column 6 to grab it mid-glide, or send sure-footed Pip), one south of the lake at (6,9).*

---

## Trail 8 — "The Peak Badger"

*The high pass narrows. A badger naps across the only path — and this one is fast. Faster than Theo.*

1. **Read the sign** at (9,8): don't try to outrun this badger — it chases faster than Theo can run.
2. **Enter the pass.** Walk both up through the gap at the west end (x=1) into the high corridor. The badger naps mid-corridor at (10,4); the old relic glints at the far east end (16,4).
3. **Bark.** Switch to Pip, walk her to about (6,4) — inside her 5-tile bark range — and press **Act**. The badger wakes, curious, and waddles over to sniff where Pip barked.
4. **Sneak.** While it's sniffing (about 6 seconds — the badger ignores Theo completely while curious, sniffing, or waddling home), switch to Theo and slip east past its empty napping spot, grab the relic at (16,4), and keep going to the campsite tent at (17,5).
5. Walk Pip east through the corridor to the tent too. Both on the campsite with the relic — trail complete.

*Fireflies: none on this trail. If the badger catches Theo, he's carried back to the trailhead but keeps the relic — bark again and retry.*

---

## Trail 9 — "The Long Climb"

*Everything they've learned, in one trail: ice, a burrow, a lever, a rope, and weight.*

1. **Cross the ice.** The frozen rows span the whole trail. Slide Theo south down any column — column 10 grabs the firefly at (10,4) on the way — to the far shore. Pip walks the ice normally.
2. **The burrow.** The rock wall (row 6) has two ways through: a stone door at (5,6) and a dark burrow at (11–13,6) — Pip-sized only. Switch to Pip and crawl the tunnel through to the far side.
3. **The lever.** Walk Pip to the brass lever at (13,7) and pull it (Act) — the stone door at (5,6) rumbles open and stays open.
4. **The rope.** Switch to Theo, walk him south through the open stone door to the anchor at (10,7), and press Act — the rope drops through the floor grate to (10,8), opening the only way down into the lower diggings.
5. **Down.** Walk both Theo and Pip down through the rope opening into the lower chamber.
6. **Weight again.** A copper door at (14,10) bars the campsite, and a stone plate waits at (8,10). Walk Theo to (13,10), east of the crate at (12,10), and push west four times — (12,10) → (11,10) → (10,10) → (9,10) → (8,10) — onto the plate. The copper door swings open and stays open.
7. Walk both through the door to the campsite tent at (16,10). Trail complete.

*Fireflies: 2 — (17,1) up top, (10,4) on the ice (grab it mid-slide down column 10).*

---

## Trail 10 — "The Last Campsite"

*The compass needle holds steady — north-east, to the last ridge. Grandfather's marker is close.*

1. **Cross the ice** like before — slide Theo south down any column to the far shore; Pip walks it.
2. **It takes two.** The copper door at (9,7) is the only way south, and its plate at (6,6) won't hold itself. Walk Pip onto the plate and press **F** (whistle) so she stays put — the door swings open.
3. **Through.** Switch to Theo and walk him south through the held-open door into the lower chamber.
4. **The heavy substitute.** A crate sits at (11,8) and a second plate waits at (14,8). Walk Theo to (10,8) and push the crate east three times — (11,8) → (12,8) → (13,8) → (14,8) — onto the far plate. The door holds itself open now.
5. **Reunite.** Walk Pip off her plate and south through the still-open door. Walk Theo to Grandfather's marker at (12,10) and pick it up — then read the last signpost at (17,10).
6. Walk both onto the campsite tent at (15,10). The map is finished.

*Fireflies: 2 — (9,4) on the ice, (6,10) beside the marker.*

## After the finale

The end screen shows your total firefly count across all ten trails. **Finish** replays the ending; **Play Again** restarts the adventure from Trail 1.
